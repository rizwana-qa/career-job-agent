### Compensation Strategy

**By target country:**
1. **Pakistan-based remote international roles** — target competitive *international* compensation, not local Pakistan QA salary bands.
2. **UAE / Dubai / Abu Dhabi** — prioritize senior, principal, lead, AI Quality Engineering, AI Testing, QA Architecture, and Quality Engineering leadership roles. Benchmark against current 2026 market rates and the actual JD, company, seniority, and benefits — not a flat number.
3. **US / EU remote** — prioritize companies that legally hire internationally or via an Employer of Record (EOR). Target globally competitive compensation appropriate for Principal, Staff, Lead, AI Quality Engineering, and AI Assurance roles.

**Undisclosed salary:** Do not reject a high-potential job solely because salary isn't disclosed. Mark it `UNKNOWN` and estimate compensation potential separately from reliable market data, rather than inventing a figure.

**Compensation classification (for future ranking logic in `src/ranking/`):** every job should be classified as one of:
- `LOW`
- `MARKET`
- `ABOVE_MARKET`
- `HIGH_POTENTIAL`
- `UNKNOWN`

**Optimize for (in priority balance, not strict order):** interview probability, compensation, career growth, international exposure, AI specialization, long-term earning potential.
